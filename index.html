I joined on chassis design and rose to captain in 2023, leading a combustion car team that competes at Supra SAEINDIA and Formula Bharat. As CAD lead I directed vehicle dynamics and structures.

  • Set up safety protocols and recruitment, and coordinated design, procurement and build to deadline.

Competition engine development in AVL CRUISE M

Starting from a supplied single-cylinder model, I developed three 2.0 L competition engines: naturally aspirated, turbocharged with an intercooler, and a hybrid with an electric motor and variable cam phasing.

  • Tuned intake and exhaust geometry, which had the largest effect on performance across all three engines.
  • Matched a turbocharger against its compressor map and worked through boost control and knock limits.
  • Benchmarked each engine against production and competition engines of the same capacity.
